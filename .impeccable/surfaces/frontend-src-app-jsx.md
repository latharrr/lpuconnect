# LpuTV app (all screens)

Scope: every screen in frontend/src/App.jsx: landing, dashboard, random matching, direct calling, chat, video, incoming-call/report plates, server wake-up. Landing is Persuade; the in-app screens are Operate and inherit the world quietly.

Audience/job: university students on phones, often at night, who want a low-stakes conversation in seconds. Action: pick gender, step in. Proof: the mechanism itself (timer, mutual enjoy, develop-on-reveal), shown on a sample strip labeled as an example. Constraints: true claims only (PRODUCT.md); socket/WebRTC logic untouched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: LpuTV is a coin-op photo booth for conversation. Two strangers, one booth, a countdown. Faces develop only when both say yes. It refuses the dark-chat-app-with-neon-accent default.

OWN-WORLD: Pleated curtain red (#a3161a) owns the landing; the booth interior is a warm near-black lit by one tungsten amber lamp (#ffb547). Strip paper (#fbfaf6) with silver emulsion (#8f8b84). Controls are enamel instruction plates and square push-buttons with small printed labels at one constant size. Third colour only for live/online. 4px unit everywhere. Display face: Big Shoulders Display, uppercase. UI/body face: Archivo.

STORY: The visitor understands it's anonymous, timed, and mutual. They believe nothing is revealed without consent, and they step in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed curtain under a chrome rod. A centred enamel plate holds the amber lamp, a huge two-line UNICONNECT, a one-line promise, three gender push-buttons and a red STEP IN at thumb height. A delivery slot below the plate feeds a tilted four-frame sample strip.

FORM: Photo Booth, candidate 7 of 7 on the ranked list; seed key 4d187ecd. Signature interaction: the match flash (white bloom), then the partner frame develops from latent fog on reveal. Motion grammar: flash, develop, feed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
