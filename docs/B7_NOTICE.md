# Notice to people affected by the B7 exposure

Send individually to each email in the backed-up v1 database (4 accounts). Owner's own account included for the record.

---

**Subject:** Your UniConnect account details were visible in our public code repository

Hi,

We're writing about your UniConnect account.

**What happened.** Between 12 March and 19 October 2026, a copy of the UniConnect user database was accidentally included in the project's public GitHub repository. It held your email address, the name from your Google account, the gender you selected, and your bio, plus the list of people you had added as friends.

**What was not included.** Message text, photos, audio or video. UniConnect never stores those.

**What we have done.** On 19 October 2026 we removed the file from the repository and its entire history, fixed the setting that let it be committed, and rebuilt the app's security (login, matching and reporting). We are also asking GitHub to clear any cached copies.

**What you can do.** Nothing is required. Because your email may have been seen, be a little more careful with unexpected emails that mention UniConnect. The new version of UniConnect started with a fresh database, so your old account no longer exists on our servers. If you want us to confirm that, or to delete the one remaining offline backup of it, reply to this email.

If you have questions or a complaint, reply to this email or contact our grievance officer: [name, email].

Sorry about this.

UniConnect
